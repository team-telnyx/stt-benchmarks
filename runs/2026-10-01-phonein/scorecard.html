Method

  • Ground truth: human utterance-level transcripts. Utterances with <UNIN/>, <INAUDIBLE_SPEECH/> or <UNSURE> markup are excluded, as are those that are empty after normalization.
  • Same audio to every engine: each clip is cut from the 16 kHz source with 150 ms padding and transcoded once to 8 kHz G.711 mu-law. Batch engines and Telnyx realtime receive 16-bit PCM decoded from that mu-law. ElevenLabs realtime receives the mu-law bytes directly.
  • Defaults on every engine. The only parameter set is the known language, on engines that accept one. Keyterm passes, where present, are labelled +keyterms and use one shared list.
  • Normalization: Whisper EnglishTextNormalizer plus unit rules (mg to milligrams and similar) and okay/OK, applied to gold and hypothesis alike (score/normalize.py).
  • Realtime: 100 ms chunks at real-time pace. The flush is the ElevenLabs commit or Telnyx CloseStream where supported. Engines without a flush control get trailing real-time silence. TTFT is the time from the first speech chunk to the first non-empty text. Final is the time from the last speech chunk to the last final transcript.
  • Term list: fixed before any engine ran. It holds Wikidata (CC0) drug and condition labels matched against gold, with common words and anatomy excluded, plus dosage and dosing patterns (datasets/primock57/terms.json).
  • Prices: ElevenLabs https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/api, Telnyx https://telnyx.com/pricing/speech-to-text, both read 2026-10-01, list pay-as-you-go.
  • Data: PriMock57, Babylon Health, CC-BY 4.0, https://github.com/babylonhealth/primock57 (Papadopoulos Korfiatis et al., ACL 2022).
